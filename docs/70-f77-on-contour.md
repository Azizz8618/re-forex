## 2. The deck (exact source)

`tests/f77/suite/base.b6`, source between `*forex` and `*execute`:

```fortran
c---- BASE: INTEGER/REAL arithmetic, counted DO, explicit FORMAT.
      PROGRAM BASE77
      N=0
      DO 10 I=1,6
         N=N+I
   10 CONTINUE
      X=1.0
      Y=X+2.0
      PRINT 100,N,X,Y
  100 FORMAT(1X,I4,2F10.2)
      STOP
      END
```

Gold (`tests/f77/suite/base.gold`): `   21      1.00      3.00`.

Chosen because it is a genuine suite deck and its whole numeric result is the
`X/Y/N` triple our contour already knows how to render (test 81): `N=21` from
the counted DO bound, `X=1.00`, `Y=3.00`.

## 3. F77PREP lowering (real run, on copies)

on copies of 2348/2048. Console records are in `f77prep_base_pass1.txt`,
parsed in `f77prep_base_records.json`.

* Line 1 (comment) -> `777001 1 14 0` plus `777004 … 20 …` long-identifier
  diagnostics (the `BASE:` colon triggers rule 14 on the comment only).
* All executable lines 2..12 -> `777001 <seq> 0 0` (**rule 0 = keep**).
* Summary: `777003 12 1` (12 logical lines, 1 rewrite, the comment diagnostic).

`prep_tools.py lower` produced `base_lowered.f77`. The only change is a DO
guard; the arithmetic subset is byte-identical to the source:

```diff
       N=0
+C@ DO 10 I=1,6 -> IF (1 .GT. 6) GO TO 90000 / 90000 CONTINUE
+      IF (1 .GT. 6) GO TO 90000
       DO 10 I=1,6
          N=N+I
    10 CONTINUE
+90000 CONTINUE
       X=1.0
       Y=X+2.0
```

So F77PREP's lowering output for the arithmetic core is `X=1.0 ; Y=X+2.0`,
which is what we feed through FRONT. The DO guard, the DO loop itself and the
`FORMAT` statement are **not** compiled by our contour (see §7).

Normalized comparison:

| Source | Console values | Numeric tuple |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Our contour (IR -> our code) | `21  01.00  03.00` | (21, 1.00, 3.00) |
| `.gold` (system `*forex`) | `   21      1.00      3.00` | (21, 1.00, 3.00) |

The three control values are **identical**. The only difference is formatting:
our fixed-width two-integer-digit GOST layout prints leading zeros and
single-space separators; FOREX right-justifies in `I4`/`F10.2` fields. This is
the same documented fixed-width caveat as test 81 §7; it is not a value error.

## 7. A/B causality table

All runs are separate fresh disk copies; `end` means `КОНЕЦ ЗАДАЧИ` present.

| Case | Mutation to our install | Console | End | Interpretation |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| `A_base` | none (baseline, IR-driven) | `21  01.00  03.00` | yes | works |
| `B_zero_desc` | zero descriptor word `body[0o25]` (RAM 0o2004) | no numeric line; `НЕТ КОНЦА БЦ.ИНФ` at `*64 2004` | yes | print path broken |
| `B_zero_code` | zero code word `body[13]` (RAM 0o1772, the ARX/STO half that writes Y) | `21  01.00  00.00` | yes | Y materialisation broken |
| `B_zero_wC` | zero data word `body[0o43]` (RAM 0o2022, wC) | `21  01.00  00.00` | yes | Y digit weights broken |
| `A_restore` | identical to A_base again | `21  01.00  03.00` | yes | restored -> works |

Conclusion: the printed `03.00` is produced by **our** descriptor, code and
data words. Zero any one of them and the value disappears or becomes `00.00`;
restore the baseline and it returns. The N and X fields are unaffected by the
Y-specific mutations, as expected from the split GOST layout.

## 8. Honest scope / limits

* Our contour compiles the **expression subset** of the real deck's lowered IR
  (`X=1.0 ; Y=X+2.0`, plus the DO bound `6` from IR). The F77PREP DO guard,
  the DO loop body and `PRINT/FORMAT` are not executed by our code; the contour
  prints its own three GOST words through `*64`. No F77LIBR procedure is called
  in the compiled output. This is the "feed a small subset through our
  FRONT.FTN/CODEGEN" option of the task, not a claim that F77LIBR was ported.
* `+ - * /` and unary minus are folded at emission from IR records; the digits
  are then materialised at run time by our own ARX words. No packed-decimal
  multiply/divide opcode was invented.
* Capacity: 2 integer + 2 fractional digits (`|v| <= 99.99`, step 0.01) and a
  2-digit bound-driven N. Fixed-width leading zeros, not byte-identical F10.2
  spacing (§6).
* Every contour console shows a post-print monitor artifact
  `disk_writei: disk 2048 is read only` / `ОШИБКА МД ЭВМ` after the value has
  already been printed and before `КОНЕЦ ЗАДАЧИ`. It appears identically in
  A_base, all three A/B mutation cases and A_restore, so it does not depend on
  the mutated word and does not affect the printed digits.
